The idea

A real bagel, done the slow way.

We're building a neighborhood bagel shop for people who care about the chew. Our dough rests overnight, then every bagel is shaped by hand, boiled, and baked until the crust crackles.

Simple ingredients, good coffee, and a schmear that's made in-house.

01

Cold-proofed overnight

A long, slow rise for deeper flavor.

02

Kettle-boiled

Gives the shiny crust and dense, chewy middle.

03

Baked every morning

Fresh from the oven, never day-old.
